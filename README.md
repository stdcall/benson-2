# Representations and Cohomology II

D. J. Benson. *Representations and Cohomology II: Cohomology of groups and
modules.* Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 31. Cambridge University
Press, first paperback edition, 1998 (first published 1991).

The book develops the cohomology of finite groups from the topological and
the algebraic side: background from algebraic topology, classifying spaces
and characteristic classes, spectral sequences, the Evens norm map and the
Steenrod algebra, varieties for modules and multiple complexes, group
actions and the Steinberg module, and local coefficients on subgroup
complexes.

This edition includes the author's later comments and corrections. The
[releases](https://github.com/stdcall/benson-2/releases) provide the book
with editorial notes, a separate list of corrections and SHA-256 checksums.
